# Style Recipe: Modern SaaS (Clean Enterprise & High Usability)

## Aesthetic Identity
Modern, trustworthy, highly accessible, and polished. Inspired by Stripe, Raycast, and Vercel.

## Design Tokens
- **Backgrounds**: `#ffffff` (Pure White), `#f8fafc` (Subtle Canvas) or `#090d16` (Midnight Dark)
- **Borders**: `#e2e8f0` (Light Slate Border), `#1e293b` (Dark Slate Border)
- **Primary Brand**: `#2563eb` (Royal Blue) or `#0ea5e9` (Sky Blue)
- **Surface Elevation**: Multi-layered card elevation with soft ambient shadows (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-xl`).
- **Typography**: Inter / Geist Sans. Crisp tabular numerals for metrics.
- **Border Radius**: 8px - 12px for modern soft corners.

## Signature Moves
- Interactive pill badges (`<span class="inline-flex items-center gap-1.5 px-3 py-1 rounded-full text-xs font-medium bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 border border-blue-200/60">...</span>`).
- Soft glassmorphism navbar (`backdrop-blur-md bg-white/80 border-b border-slate-200/80`).
- Feature cards with subtle gradient hover effects.
